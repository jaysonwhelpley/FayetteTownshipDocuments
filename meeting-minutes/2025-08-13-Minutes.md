# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, August 13, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD AUGUST 13, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Bill Sarge, Toby Neff, Jeremy Culbertson, Becky Lock.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve. Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: Jeremy Culbertson was present to discuss his property taxes. His property is in both Fermanagh and Fayette Townships. All his building are in Fayette Township. He would like his taxes changed to Fayette Township. Chris Strawser stated he talked to Mike Hower about the situation. He stated an agreement would have to be made with Fermanagh Township in order to change. Seth Mosebey is going to discuss with Fermanagh Township.

Old Business: None

New Business: Bill Sarge was present with the Timothy Rickenbaugh plan. The purpose of the plan is to add Lot A 0.195 acres to Lot 2, to the land of W. Geraldine Rickenbaugh Estate from Lot 1, the land of Timothy & Yvonne Rickenbaugh. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion. Craig Strawser second, with Chris Strawser making it unanimous.

Green Light Go bids were opened, the bids were as follows: Herr Signal & Lighting Co.-$205,144.00, TRA Electric Inc.-$258,055.00, Kuharchik Construction Inc.-$216,125.00. The bid was awarded to Herr Signal & Lighting Co. contingent upon final review of the bid package. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Chris Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the purchase of a new laptop for Craig. The version of Windows on his computer is no longer supported and not able to be upgraded. The secretary got a price from High Tech Hobbies for $700.00 to $800.00. It was decided to purchase from High Tech Hobbies since we use them for support for all our computers. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Chris Strawser second.

William Nale reported issuing six permits. Mike Dressler-storage,renew permit for house, Cindy Swartz-roof solar panels, Dale Brubaker/Trinity-roof solar panels, Larry Deppen-pool, Gerald Houtz ground solar, Hunter Gingrich-new home. The square of McAlisterville being cleaned up was mentioned. Lonnie Snyder pulled his dump trailer from the Livingston property.

Lost Creek Community Park: The fans installed in the men's restroom seem to be helping with the moisture problem. Fans will be installed in the women's restroom after they are closed for the year. Chris Strawser installed a new ground fault in the #2 pavilion.

Oakland Mills Sewer: The Evangelical Methodist Church has hooked up to the sewer as of July. The supervisors decided to charge the residential rate of $50.00 per month to the church. The secretary is to bill the church for July, August, September. William Nale will provide the billing information. Cummins emailed information regarding a repair needed on the generator after inspecting. Cummins is suggesting replacing the backup battery. The supervisors discussed replacing the battery ourselves rather than have Cummins replace it. Chris Strawser is going to call them. Chris Strawser is going to get a letter ready to inform Patricia Bowersox that her sewer service is going to be shut off 30 days after receipt of the letter.

Lucas Parkes: The grant for Leonard Rd expires in 2028. The supervisors and Lucas are going to meet with Steve/Lost Creek Ag & the church sometime in October to discuss the Leonard Rd project. The grant for the Rockland Road project for $2.2 million was submitted on July 31, 2025. Lucas is going to look into vacating Quarry Rd. Lucas stated he reviewed the JCS plan, it should be presented at the October meeting for the supervisors approval.

The supervisors discussed Dennis Lukens retirement effective September 30, 2025, and the hiring of Whitey Frontz effective October 1, 2025. The supervisors also discussed possibly adding a third full-time employee since the part-time position was never filled and someone will be needed for the park maintenance next year.

The supervisors approved and signed the revised Granny Flats Ordinance 2025-3. The motion to approve was made by Robert Barton Jr. Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting September 10, 2025 at 6pm.
