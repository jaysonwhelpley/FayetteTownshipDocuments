# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, July 9, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JULY 9, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey, Brent Musser, Becky Lock, Rachel Rowles, Gerald Fulkroad, Jim King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: None

New Business: Brent Musser was present with the MAJA Water Storage Tank/Well development plan for conditional approval pending DEP approval of WQ NPDES permit. The supervisors gave conditional approval with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton, Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the Capital Blue Cross renewal package. The renewal package has an increase of $477.35 per month with the new rate being $3,263.61. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The Granny Flats ordinance was reviewed, a few changes were made. Seth will have the final ordinance drafted and advertised for the August meeting.

The contract for ATMR/Jeff Hoover was reviewed. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve the new contract, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Chris will take care of getting signatures.

The purchase of a new utility trailer was discussed. Craig got quotes from Hilly Ridge, Fayette Trailer. It was decided to get quotes from Hoover's and Tractor Supply before deciding.

William Nale reported issuing two permits. Timothy Rickenbaugh-40 x 32 barn with 16 x 12 lean to roof, Ivan Petersheim-12 x 20 storage shed. Seth will check for UCC ordinance, where filed. If he can't find one he will draft a new ordinance.

Lost Creek Park: Fans were installed in the men's restroom to deal with a moisture issue. Fans are ordered for the women's restroom.

Oakland Mills Sewer: The generator was serviced on 7/9/25. The hookup fee and quarterly fee for the Evangelical Methodist Church will be discussed at the August meeting.

The supervisors discussed applying for the DCED Multimodal grant for the Rockland Road project. We will be applying for $2,000,000.00 with a $200,000.00 match. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve the application, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Resolution 2025-3 for the grant request was approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Lucas Parkes stated the Green Light Go was advertised for bids in the Juniata Sentinel July 9, 2025 and July 16, 2025. Bid packages were sent to several companies that typically bid on this type of projects. Bids will be opened at the August 13th meeting. Lucas will review bills submitted, once he approves the township will submit payment to PennDOT. PennDOT will then reimburse the township. Lucas reported nothing new on the Leonard Road grant. Lucas is checking Quarry Road to prepare for vacating the road.

Rachel Rowles was present to discuss possibly renting the former JVB bank building for a general store. The supervisors stated they would need to determine what structural updates would need done to bring the building up to code. Also the costs involved would have to be determined before a decision could be made. The supervisors are going to meet with Lucas to discuss all updates that would be necessary.

Gerald Fulkroad was present to discuss the former Elsasser property. He questioned whether he could install solar panels. The supervisors discussed information from our Solar Ordinance, which prohibits solar panel farms on agriculture zoned properties. The supervisors told Gerald Fulkroad if he wanted to pursue the solar panel farm, the matter would have to go through a zoning hearing with the zoning hearing board.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting August 13, 2025 at 6pm.
