# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, October 8, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD OCTOBER 8, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawer, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Becky Lock, Betty Ryan, Mike Ryan, Tina Ryan, Sonya Nace, Jason Nace, Kenneth Good, Sally Fogleman, Cathy Shellenberger, Ken, Holly, Kenley Maxwell, Lowell Shirk, Michael Shirk, Harold Fogleman, Jim King, Spencer McLaughlin.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Under old business, Jay Graybill needs to fill out an extension request for the zoning hearing since he will be out of the area when it should be held.

Christopher Strawser stated the township needs to establish a Floodplain Management Ordinance. Failure to do so will jeopardize future claims from township residents. Items suggested for the ordinance, fuel tanks should be anchored, storage shed either anchored or the capability for the water to run through. Christopher Strawser is going to print the model ordinance for Seth Mosebey to review. The requirements are 15 hrs of video instruction and a test to qualify. The supervisors will look at next month.

The supervisors approved and signed the MMO payment of $10,645.00. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors approved & signed the VFRA payment of $22,514.56. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors approved and signed the Juniata Christian School plans for the parking lot/soccer field project. Craig Strawser made the motion to approve, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing four permits. Dan/Tricia Seaman-pre-fabricated 12x20 shed, David Troyer-12x24 storage building, Jesse Weaver-32x36 storage building, Jefferson Varner-lean to on carport.

Lost Creek Community Park: Email from Tammy Brackbill concerning the America250PA event June 13, 2026. She had a few questions about kitchen availability, garbage, parking, insurance, fireworks. The secretary is going to send the responses, the kitchen isn't available, the rest her questions the same rules would apply as the carnival. The supervisors are going to meet with Rose Auker, Tammy Brackbill concerning placing the old Fayette Elementary School sign somewhere in the park. The supervisors expressed concerns about placing it by the war memorial, citing the possibility of future war memorials being placed there. Craig Strawser is going to set up a time to meet next week.

Oakland Mills: Christopher Strawser and Whitey Frontz cleaned the sludge at the plant. Cory Fronk stated they should keep up with that as it could cause problems if not kept cleaned.

All three supervisors will be attending the Juniata County Association of Township Officials convention.

Lucas Parkes stated the Green Light Go project drawings are being reviewed for approval. Site Kickoff meeting for placement of the pole possibly January or February. Herr Signal & Lighting should have the pole for the project ordered. Leonard Rd grant awards are on hold due to the Federal Government shutdown. Lucas stated he is working on the Quarry Rd vacation project. Craig Strawser suggested Ben Diem be kept in the loop on what is taking place since he owns property near the project. Christopher Strawser stated he would like to apply for a grant to demo the former JVB bank building, the grant applications are due November 30, 2025. Lucas stated he is working on the guidelines for hookups at the Oakland Mills Sewer, he is hoping to advertise in January and sign in February.

The supervisors discussed the advertisement for bids to sell the used Western V Snow plow. When it was purchased it was intended to be mounted on the 2015 Silverado. It was never installed. It was decided to advertise the plow for sale by bid. Bids will be opened at the November 12, 2025 meeting.

The supervisors discussed future equipment purchases since the proposed budget will be reviewed next month.

Kenneth Good was present to discuss constructing a 60x96 commercial building on his property to do repairs on ag & industrial equipment. The property is currently zoned Agricultural. He was given an application for a zoning hearing.

Sonya Nace was present along with several other residents of Fayette Township to discuss the rezoning of the Lowell Shirk property. Lowell Shirk intends to construct a new building for his Pallet Grocery business which is now located on Rockland Road. Sonya presented the concerns of the neighboring residents about the project. They are concerned about the loss of the farmland, traffic issues. Lowell Shirk spoke during the meeting to explain his plans for the property/store. The supervisors will be holding a public hearing at the November 12, 2025 meeting to make the decision on the rezoning.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting November 12, 2025 at 6 pm.
