# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, May 14, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD MAY 14, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Craig Strawser, Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Bill Sarge, Bill Wright, Becky Lock, Jim King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Jessica Guyer America 250PA celebration. The supervisors will discuss at the next meeting whether to charge a fee.

New Business: Bids for the 2025 paving project were opened. The bids were as follows: Glenn O. Hawbaker-$262,455.25, New Enterprise-$283,259.20, Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc-$258,717.05. The bid was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc. in the amount of $258,717.05. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. A copy of the bid results is attached.

Bids were also opened for the materials bid. The bid was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. A copy of the bid results is attached.

Bill Wright was present with the Cody/Cortney Shellenberger plan on Stine Lane to Samuel Weiand. Cody is selling to Andrew Frymoyer. The purpose of the plan is to add a 0.362 & a 0.944 acre lot addition to Lot A. The residue will remain woodland use. The waivers were approved with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton, Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Bill Sarge was present with the Randy/Beulah Strawser plan. The purpose of this plan is to consolidate Losts A,B,C,D, and E into Lot 1 of 1.334 acres. Lot 2 of 0.0467 acres is an existing lot that won't be changed, and isn't part of this consolidation. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing six permits. Richard Graybill, Jr.-30x32 detached garage, Daniel Huffman-driveway, Deer Ridge Const/Trent Fisher-single story addition, Matthew Moyer-12x24 shed, Randy Naylor-28x32 pole building, Timothy Strong-30x26 carport. William Nale reported he extended Aaron Swarey's permit. The Livingston/Snyder situation was discussed. The supervisors are meeting with them on Friday, May 16th. The hearing will be May 30, 2025. Christopher Strawser will take new pictures to show any progress with the cleanup.

The supervisors discussed the possibility of vacating Quarry Rd. Seth Mosebey will look into what is involved in the process.

Lost Creek Community Park: Christopher Strawser stated he was contacted by Mrs. Hoover about the mowing of their property. The supervisors will check the situation on Friday, May 16th. The secretary reported the Juniata County Helping Hands benefit is Friday, June 13, 2025.

Oakland Mills: Information regarding Patricia Bowersox's account was given to Seth Mosebey to proceed with collection. Bud Swartz from Commonwealth Code is supposed to send Patricia Bowersox a notice to vacate her property since she never got a final inspection to receive the Certificate of Occupancy. Craig Strawser reported a notice of Sheriff's sale was on the door of the Terry Colyer residence. Evangelical Methodist Church hookup to the sewer was discussed. Craig Strawser stated he talked to Doug Elsasser, he is okay with going across the back of his lot.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas wasn't present. Christopher Strawser reported speaking with Lucas concerning the Green Light Go project. Lucas reported the plans are being reviewed by PennDOT. The project should be ready for bidding by July. Leonard Rd project wasn't discussed. Lucas is getting information on the Granny Flats to Seth Mosebey so he can prepare the ordinance in the next two weeks.

Jim King was present to discuss the 100th Anniversary celebration for the Fayette Fire Company. It will be held at the park on September 20th, and September 21st. They will be holding a parade September 20th at 1:00 pm with no raindate. The supervisors stated the Fayette Fire Co. won't be charged a fee for the use of the park. Jim also discussed the bridge behind the firehouse. Craig Strawser will check with Glenn Kerstetter, the supervisors will look at the bridge on Friday, May 16th.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting June 11, 2025 at 6 pm.
