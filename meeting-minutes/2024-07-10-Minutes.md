# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, July 10, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JULY 10, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Nick Jacoson, Pam Herzing, David Groff.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: Pam Herzing was present to discuss her property on Main Street. She questioned why part of her property is zoned commercial and part residential. The supervisors are going to look into the matter. Nick Jacobson State Representative candidate was present to discuss local government concerns with the supervisors.

Old Business: Christopher Strawser reported the fryer that doesn't work was removed from the kitchen. The #3 pavilion water drainage issues are being looked into to determine what needs to be done to correct the problem. Spouting Unlimited is going to check what spouting needs installed. Hope to start the work next week.

New Business: Bids for the paving project on Pine/Maple Street were opened. Glenn O. Hawbaker-$48,467.00, New Enterprise-$49,983.34, Jay Fulkroad & Sons-$43,908.34, Route 333 Supplies-$51,296.00, Mike Cleck Paving-$56,500.00. The bid was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons in the amount of $43,908.34. Copies of the bids results are attached. The supervisors discussed issues in the past with Fulkroad's. It was decided to send them an email with some conditions that need to be met on the project. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to award the bid, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

David Groff was present to discuss his proposal for additional trailers at the former Brandt Trailer Park in McAlisterville. The supervisors approved his plan. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed and approved the Sewage Ordinance 2-2024 as advertised. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The contract with ATMR(Jeff Hoover) has been renewed for 2024-2025.

The Capital Blue Cross renewal package was reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing three permits. Matthew VonFricken-addition to connect house and garage, enclose patio, Michael/Barb Linetty-cabin-signed affidavit, C. Scott Edmiston-garage. Craig mentioned Ehrenzeller's have a storage building on their property, he is going to keep an eye on the activity.

Lost Creek Park: The supervisors discussed the quote from DuMor for a park bench. It was decided not to replace the bench at this time.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant: Christopher Strawser reported Sippel took care of some of the punch list items. The retention pond needs the volume calculated. Russ Bowersox is going to look for his copy of the letter Doug Elsasser signed for the church to access the sewer. Cummins serviced the plant generator on 7/10/24.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas is supposed to have a drawing with the changed design of the Green Light Go Project ready for a meeting with PennDOT. The Leonard Rd grant is to be submitted by July 31, 2024.

The supervisors approved a donation of $120.00 to the Juniata County Library. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed Sunset Road trees that need trimmed or removed. The township will pay for two on township right of way, Barbara Peachey will pay for the rest.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting August 14, 2024 at 7 pm.
