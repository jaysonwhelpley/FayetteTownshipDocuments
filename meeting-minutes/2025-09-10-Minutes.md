# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, September 10, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD SEPTEMBER 10, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey, Jay Graybill, Lowell Shirk, Mark Amig, Bill Hummel.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Craig Strawser stated the battery for the generator at Oakland Mills Sewer Plant was replaced. Christopher Strawser is going to call Cummins and let them know we replaced it. Christopher Strawser confirmed the retirement of Dennis Lukens effective September 30, 2025.

New Business: Lucas Parkes was present to get agreement for Herr's bid Green Light Go paperwork signed.

Mark Amig and Bill Hummell were present to discuss the Emergency Management Coordinator. Mark Amig questioned if we had a COOP plan. Information requested for the plan is for succession of supervisors, a list of equipment should the county need for an emergency. Self assessment questions if we have a flood plain ordinance. It includes a two hour online meeting. If the township doesn't complete the training they may come do an audit. Mark Amig will email Christopher Strawser the information that needs completed.

Lowell Shirk was present to discuss the rezoning of property on Tennis Park Road from agriculture to commercial. The property already joins commercial property. Seth will have to advertise and an amendment will have to be made to the ordinance. We should be able to discuss at the October meeting.

The supervisors reviewed the PSATS nominee for the Health Trust, Pension Trust, Unemployment Trust. Cory Adams was the nominee. The supervisors approved Cory Adams. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the invoice from Jay Fulkroad & Sons for the paving project. The amount of the invoice is $254,329.53. Town Ridge was 2" instead of 2 ½" which Craig stated was his fault. Christopher Strawser questioned if we wanted to dispute the amount. Craig Strawser went over all the issues we had with the project with Matt Kenepp to justify future projects we bid not to be awarded to Fulkroad's. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve the payment, Christopher Strawser second, Craig Strawser objected.

William Nale reported issuing five permits. Bradley Huffman- 14x48 shed, Frederick Longenecker-20x40x14 car port, Aaron Swarey-barn/buggy shed, Michael Brubaker-30x30 garage, Michael Brubaker-wraparound porch, William Nale-12x24 car port. The supervisors discussed Michael Brubaker request to put some cottages on the property at 96 Stone Ridge Lane, McAlisterville. Michael Brubaker was instructed to contact Herb Stoner regarding the sewage system requirements. The supervisors feel it won't be allowed.

Jay Graybill was present to request his property be rezoned from agricultural to commercial for the purpose of constructing a welding shop. Half of the property is Monroe Township, half is in Fayette Township. Christopher Strawser stated we can't spot change the zoning. The request will have to go through a zoning hearing with the zoning hearing board. The fee to start to zoning hearing process is $1,000.00. The adjoining land owners would need to be notified of the hearing. The zoning hearing needs to be advertised by Don Zagurskie, he will contact the adjoining land owners, advertise the hearing, set the date for the hearing. The hearing needs to be within sixty days of the application.

Lost Creek Community Park: The secretary reported all the bills for the carnival are in except the sewer/water bill. The carnival committee has $400.00 credit to apply to the charges. The sewer/water bill will come the beginning of October.

Oakland Mills: Christopher Strawser stated the plant needs cleaned beginning of October. He reported the numbers are good. Patricia Bowersox has until the end of September, then she will be notified by the sheriff that she will be shut off within ten days. Terry Colyer's sheriff sale is scheduled for September 26, 2025. The township is going to charge a hookup fee to the Evangelical Methodist Church.

Christopher Strawser stated he wants to apply for a DCED grant to demolish the bank building. Brad Ferster from Delaware Township knows someone interested if we demolish the building.

Craig Strawser stated National Gunite is quoting culvert repairs on Sunset Rd, and Benner Rd.

Christopher Strawser stated the supervisors should start thinking about equipment purchase/projects for the budget.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting October 8, 2025 at 6 pm.
