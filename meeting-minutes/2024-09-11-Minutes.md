# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, September 11, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD SEPTEMBER 11, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey, Daniel Auker, Brent Musser.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: None

New Business: Daniel Auker was present with his plan from Wright Surveying. The plan is for a lot addition to Keith Nipple for his pallet business. The waivers were approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr., making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Axis Surveying/Brent Musser was present with the Roy King plan. Sewage testing has been done. From a 10 acre lot, he is keeping 2 acres, 7.98 acres going to his son. The waivers were approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the PSATS ballots for trustees for Health, Pension, Unemployment and voted for the two delegate options. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing two permits. Jonathan Wileman-24x32 pole building, Roger Winey-16x27 covered porch addition. William Nale reported speaking with Bob Fleisher on the corner of Dunn Valley Road about operating and antique gun shop from is basement. He has the room in the supervisors approve. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Steve Ehrenzeller's on Fairview Road got a permit in August for 2 storage buildings. One is being used for his wife's business, the supervisors will keep an eye on the situation.

Lost Creek Community Park: The supervisors discussed the carnival fee/expenses. The secretary reported the sewer/water bill won't be available until October 1, 2024. After that is received the totals will be available. Tammy Brackbill has requested a check be issued for whatever credit remains rather than holding it towards next year's carnival. For now the supervisors would like to keep the fee at $4,000.00. Christopher Strawser would like to attend a carnival committee meeting to discuss some issues.

Oakland Mills: Christopher Strawser reported there wasn't a significant increase in flows during the latest rain storm. Lucas is going to work on a mandatory connection ordinance/application for new users. Included in the ordinance will be specifications for pipe, grinders, etc.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas reported the meeting with PennDot concerning the Green Light Go project was held. Lucas stated the engineer from French's and Jonathan Tate(PennDOT) came up with another option. The options are purchasing the bank with plans of future upgrades in the intersection or purchasing the parking lot across from the bank and installing a double mast arm post for the traffic signals and control panel. The pedestrian crossings would be changed.

Christopher Strawser reported the Port Royal EMS meeting discussion on the Per Capita Tax. It was discovered the maximum amount allowed is $10.00.

The supervisors discussed a road study on Musser/McMeen roads to change the stop sign. An ordinance would need to be passed to install a stop sign.

Craig Strawser stated he contacted Alpha Space about painting lines on the south side of 35, the roads that were tar/chipped this summer, and possibly Pine/Maple which was paved.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting October 9, 2024 at 7 pm.
