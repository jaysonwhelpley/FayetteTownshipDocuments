# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, June 11, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JUNE 11, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Becky Lock, Virgina Heikes.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: The supervisors met to look at the bridge from the fire company to the park. Christopher Strawser talked to Glenn Kerstetter. They are planning on pulling the deck off, replacing the deck and installing new beams. They should be able to go ahead with the updates.

New Business: William Nale reported issuing three permits. Tim Varner-10x16 building, Elvin Geigley-addition to home(inlaw quarters), Randall Brubaker- ground solar panels. Kenneth Good wants to build a garage to repair equipment, the property is 4.5 acres zoned agriculture. The supervisors will need more details before approving. Lowell Shirk questioned if agriculture land could be changed to commercial. William Nale suggested he send a letter describing what he wants to do. If it borders commercial we could possibly rezone. A property owner on Town Ridge questioned putting a trailer on her property they use for camping.

The supervisors discussed vacating Quarry Road with Seth Mosebey. Seth suggested getting Lucas Parkes to survey before proceeding. Current homeowners would need access.

The supervisors discussed the request from Zito Media to install telecom poles on McMeen Road. The supervisors will meet with Zito Media.

Lost Creek Community Park: Jessica Guyer's request to use the park for her America 250PA celebration June 2026 was discussed. The supervisors decided they wouldn't charge a fee. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The carnival fee for 2025 was discussed. It was decided to keep the fee at $4,000.00.

Oakland Mills Sewer: Christopher Strawser reported Doug Elsasser has agreed to access across the back of his property so the Evangelical Methodist Church can hookup to the sewer system. Seth Mosebey will prepare a letter for Doug/Wendy to sign. We have one grinder pump which was left over from the original project that can be used. Several options for the church hookup were discussed. Lucas Parkes has submitted the discharge permit to DEP. He hasn't received comments yet. Seth Mosebey reported he has to confirm property owners before filing a lien for collection of Patricia Bowersox's account.

Lucas Parkes: Green Light Go project has been approved by PennDOT. Lucas is preparing the bid package for advertisement with bids being opened at our August meeting. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to go ahead with the bidding process, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Christopher Strawser will contact Lucas concerning the Juniata Christian School project. Lucas informed Christopher Strawser there is a multimodal grants available. He thought we might be able to apply for funds to update routes to pallet shops. They will discuss when meeting with Lucas. Lucas is supposed to get the Granny Flats information to Seth later this week. Lucas and the supervisors need to meet with the church & Steve(Lost Creek Ag) to discuss the Leonard Road project. Lucas is reviewing the Aaron Swarey plan.

The supervisors will meet with Charles Tini Friday morning around 9:00-9:30.

Christopher Strawser stated Livingston has about half of his property cleaned up. He has 2 ½ weeks until the hearing.

Virgina Heikes was present to discuss her property in the Cheswyk development. Her residence is close to one the retaining ponds. She is concerned the ground is eroding too close to her property. The supervisors responded since it is a private development it isn't a township issue.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting July 9, 2025 at 6 pm.
