# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, November 12, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD NOVEMBER 12, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Craig Strawser, Christopher Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebery, Brent Musser, Brad Kerstetter, Deanna Stong, Valerie Fogleman, Harold Fogleman, Jeff Freyermuth, Mark Cramer, Betty Ryan, James King, Sonya Nace, Holly Maxwell, Dan Auker, Lowell Shirk, Jim Knepp, Jim King, Mike Ryan, Becky Lock.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve. Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: The secretary was instructed to prepare a check for the remaining $16,000.00 to Port Royal EMS for the December 10, 2025 meeting. The zoning hearing for Jay Graybill/JK Assets Holdings, LLC is November 19, 2025 at 7:00 pm.

Brent Musser was present with the Lowell Shirk plan. It is a lot addition adding 30.74 acres to exiting 8 acres, it will be used for hunting, fishing no development. The waivers were approved with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Bids Snow Plow: No bids were received. It was decided to set it out front and put a for sale sign on it.

The supervisors reviewed the proposed 2026 budget. The proposed budget was adopted as presented. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the Tax Levy Resolution for 2026. Resolution 2025-4 was adopted with no increase in taxes. Craig Strawser made the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing four permits. Chris Hoffman-30x60 pole building, Trent Fisher-home addition/covered deck, Ian Ramp-20x36x14 garage addition, Pam Herzing-roof mount solar panels.

Lost Creek Community Park: The meeting regarding the Fayette School sign being installed somewhere in the park has been moved to spring. The supervisors stated they don't want it by the veteran's memorial area in anticipation additional memorials being placed there. The park restrooms have been winterized.

Oakland Mills: A check was received from a delinquent account. A payment is supposed to be made the beginning of December. A third check the end of December, with the balance paid by the beginning of the year.

Lucas Parkes: Green Light Go project construction has begun. Everything is done, waiting for the mast/signal to arrive some in January/February. Lucas suggested waiting to hear from Herr Lighting regarding invoice payment. Leonard Road project waiting to get together with Lost Creek Ag. Quarry Road started pulling a plan together, Lucas wants to talk to Seth. Rockland Road grant was submitted. The LSA Grants haven't been decided because of the government shutdown. The LSA grant for the bank demo needs to be submitted by November 30, 2025. The supervisors voted on applying for the LSA Grant Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion for the Resolution 2025-5, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making unanimous.

Rezoning of Lowell Shirk property hearing was handled by Seth Mosebey. A court reporter was present. Valerie Fogleman and Sonya Nace both asked to be on the agenda to speak. After listening to several residents comments and questions directed to the supervisors, Lowell Shirk, and Brad Kerstetter/Juniata County Planning Commission, Seth Mosebey/Township Solicitor. The supervisors voted in favor of the rezoning. Craig Strawser made the motion, Christopher Strawser second, with Robert Barton Jr. making it unanimous.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting December 10, 2025 at 6 pm.
