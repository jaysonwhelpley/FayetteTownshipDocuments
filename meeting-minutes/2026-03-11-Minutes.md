# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, March 11, 2026

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD MARCH 11, 2026 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisorss held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Herb Stoner, Steve Ehrenzeller, Penny Ehrenzeller, Jim King, George King, Mike Ryan.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Marcellus Shale Legacy Grant application-Christopher Strawser will fill out. Applications are due March 31, 2026. Patricia Bowersox-supposed to pay $200.00 March 13th.

New Business: Steve and Penny Ehrenzeller were present with a subdivision plan most of the property is in Delaware Township(they have already approved the plan), woodland is in Fayette Township. The supervisors reviewed the plan and gave conditional approval pending DEP approval. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Craig Strawser will contact Matt Kenepp(PennDOT) to make an appointment towards the end of the month to check roads for recommendation which roads to pave.

Herb Stoner was present to discuss the granny flats on the Simon Byler property. Seth Mosebey advised Herb Stoner to send a letter informing him they have thirty days to disconnect the sewer. If Herb's letter doesn't work Seth will send a letter. Mike Ryan was present to discuss whether he would be allowed to install a trailer on his property for his son and daughter-in-law.

The trailer would be temporary for approximately three years or less if they find property to buy. The supervisors stated they would not be allowed under the current zoning ordinance. Christopher Strawser addressed the letter we received concerning a property on Mountain Rd. which someone was living in without sewer/water with Herb.

The supervisors reviewed Resolution 4-2026 pertaining to tax collector fees charged for providing duplicate tax bills. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the quote from National Gunite for the Sunset Rd. culvert rehab. Craig is going to contact Lucas Parkes about coming to check it out.

The supervisors reviewed the paperwork from Martson Law concerning Seth Mosebey separating from the group. The supervisors decided to keep Seth Mosebey as the township solicitor, Mosebey Law Group, LLC. Robert Barton made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the traffic light maintenance agreement from Khuarchik Construction. It was decided to renew the contract. Khuarchik needs to be notified about the new traffic light. We also need to check Herr Signal & Lighting about warranty on the traffic light. Craig Strawser made the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing three permits. Scott Dunn-30x30 addition to home w/16x12 breezeway connecting to 30x30 new garage, Nathan Snyder-Pole shed/garage, Clayton Spade-42x75 garage.

George King presented an updated list of the Fayette Township Fire Police. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Lost Creek Community Park: The supervisors have decided to hire Phil Holesapple for park maintenance at $16.50 per hour. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The supervisors also discussed the tree across from the park that needs cut. Craig Strawser stated Jerry Auker would like the tree when cut down.

Oakland Mills Sewer: Christopher Strawser stated there are issues with the DO probe not running. When the screen was reset it was working.

Lucas Parkes: Green Light Go project-Herr is supposed to be on sight next week. Leonard Rd.-he is concerned with the design sight distance to Route 35. Quarry Rd-Lucas is finalizing paperwork for Seth Mosebey.

The supervisors accepted the letter of resignation from Beverly Richmond to be effective June 10, 2026. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to accept, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Christopher Strawser mentioned getting Bev a gift card or something in appreciation for her service as tax collector. The supervisors will be appointing Lori Diehl as interim tax collector at the April meeting. Lori Diehl is going to have her office at the township building. The supervisors will meet with her to discuss how to set the office up.

Craig Strawser brought up the discussion of the repairs needed for the cinder bin. The supervisors decided to contact Lucas Parks to look at the cinder bin for his input on the repairs.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting April 8, 2026 at 6 pm.
