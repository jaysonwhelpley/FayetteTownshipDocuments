# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, February 11, 2026

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

181 BUNKERTOWN RD FEBRUARY 11, 2026 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Herb Stoner, David Graybill, Phil Holesapple, Jessica Guyer, Tammy Brackbill.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved with the correction of Mike Ryan to Jason Nace meeting with the supervisors. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Herb Stoner was present to check in with the township if they had any concerns. He stated he never got notified the septic system was abandoned on the Ehrenzeller Road project. Herb is going to send the resident a letter.

The supervisors resigned the plans for the Juniata County Christian School. There was something printed wrong on the original plans we signed.

The supervisors discussed the quote for the Sunset Road culvert rehabilitation from National Gunite. The quote was $75,483.00, they will need to use a pump to close off water flow. Craig Strawser got a quote from L/B Water for the pipe it would take if the work was done by the township. Just the pipe would be approximately $19,000.00. If the township did the work, permits would be required, plus the time/equipment involved in the repairs. Craig stated he didn't get the quote on the Benner Road culvert. The supervisors decided to discuss the quote with Lucas Parkes before making a decision.

David Graybill and Phil Holesapple attended to ask questions about the Lost Creek Community Park maintenance job that was advertised. The work that is required of the position was discussed. The position is part-time, not paid through payroll, a 1099 will be issued at year-end. Christopher Strawser is going to set up meetings with any job applicants for next week.

Jessica Guyer and Tammy Brackbill were present to update the supervisors on the progress of the America250PA event they are planning at the Lost Creek Community Park on June 13, 2026. They presented the supervisors with of list of committed vendors/activities/food to date. Enos Yoder stated it would be okay to let the tent up an extra day. The supervisors committed to providing the BBQ pits and restroom supplies/cleaning at no charge for the event. Jessica Guyer discussed the quote she received from Enders Insurance for the event. Christopher Strawser is going to check with Enders Insurance if the cost would be cheaper through the township. They are going to get a banner to hang in town. The supervisors suggested by the Lutheran Church. A county wide mailer will be sent out to residents.

The application for the Marcellus Shale Legacy Grant was discussed. Christopher Strawser will fill out the application. The deadline to apply is March 31, 2026.

Ordinance 2002-4 concerning tax collector fees was discussed with Seth Mosebey. Seth is going to review the ordinance and a sample resolution from Milford Township. He will have something for next month's meeting.

William Nale reported issuing three permits. DEC-Tim Rickenbaugh-demolition of house, Ben Schrack-32x26 garage addition to barn, Lewis Yoder/Paul Weaver-30x30 lunchroom, Sustainable Energy/Gary Beatty-roof mounted solar.

Christopher Strawser stated he needs to get Lepley Electric to check an issue with the UV light.

Christopher Strawser also spoke with Patricia Bowersox concerning her outstanding balance for sewer services. She promised $200.00 on February 14th, $200.00 on February 27th, with the remaining balance to be paid in March.

Lucas Parkes wasn't in attendance, but Christopher Strawser spoke with him. He stated he is working on the final drawings for the Leonard Road project. He is sending Seth Mosebey information on the Quarry Road project for review. The LSA grant for the bank demolition won't be awarded until September.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting March 11, 2026 at 6 pm.
