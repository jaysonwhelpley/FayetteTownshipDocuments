# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, June 12, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JUNE 12, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Brent Musser.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: None

New Business: Axis Surveying was present with the David Renno plan and the Michael Troyer plan. The purpose of the Renno plan is to subdivide Lot 1 from TMP 03-17-29 to create one residential building lot. The waivers were approved with Craig Strawser making the motion to approve, Robert Barton Jr second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The purpose of the Troyer plan is to subdivide Lot 1 from TMP 03-02-28 to create a single family residential building lot. The waivers were given conditional approval upon DEP approval. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was given conditional approval upon DEP approval. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The bids for the tar/chip project were opened. The tar/chip project was awarded to Russell Standard in the amount of $123,089.34. A copy of the bid results is attached. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to award the bid to Russell Standard, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The materials bid package was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc., the total bid package amount was $153,500.00. A copy of the bid results is attached. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to award the bid to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc., Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the rehab of the culverts on Pine/Maple, and Peoples Rd. Craig Strawser researched the possibility of doing the work ourselves. It was decided there were too many obstacles on Pine/Maple with the water/sewer, electric, and telephone lines, cost of the permit to justify doing the work ourselves. It was decided to accept the proposal from National Gunite(Co-Stars) in the amount of $77,377.00. Craig Strawser is going to call to get the project started.

The supervisors discussed the paving of Pine/Maple. It was decided to bid the project. Craig is going to call Matt Kenepp to get the bid process started.

William Nale reported issuing 8 permits(1-free). Mike Eby-replace existing deck, Aaron Swarey-barn & buggy shed, new home, Cliff Swartwood-deck behind house, Urie Byler-40x44 horse/buggy barn, Jeff Yoder-addition to home, Charles Tini-repair fire damage to apartments, Robert Swartz-above ground pool, Jonathan Wileman-retaining wall. The request from Missy Culbertson for permission to use the Fayette Fire Company's kitchen to make baked goods was discussed. Missy stated she needs township approval. The question was asked where she would be selling the goods from. Christopher Strawser was going to contact her. William Nale questioned the Bowersox property in Oakland Mills. Christopher Strawser stated they haven't done anything yet. The former Brandt Trailer Park was discussed. The new owner wants to purchase additional property to install a bigger trailer. The supervisors stated anything new he installs has to meet zoning requirements.

Lost Creek Community Park- Christopher Strawser stated they will be taking the fryer out that doesn't work. They discussed possible work at pavilion #3.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant-Christopher Strawser reported everything is running okay. It was decided to have Seth Mosebey follow up with letter sent to Sippel for punch list items.

Lucas Parks-The green light go project was discussed. PennDot has agreed to an extension for the project. However, Lucas stated with the added issues of the ADA ramps, and right of way issues he feels PennDot needs to reevaluate to project because the cost to do everything PennDot is asking is going to be well over the grant amount. Lucas stated by next week French Engineering will have information available. Lucas will be in contact with the supervisors on the plan moving forward. Lucas reported he is moving ahead with the permits for the Leonard Road project.

Seth Mosebey is going to move forward with the Sewage Ordinance, 10 acre exemption. He will advertise the ordinance for the July meeting.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting July 10, 2024 at 7 pm.
