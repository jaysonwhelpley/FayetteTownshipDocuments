# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, January 8, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JANUARY 8, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Craig Strawser, Sharon Lukens, William Nale.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Kurachik worked on the traffic signals, working okay so far. Christopher Strawser presented a letter to Patricia Bowersox concerning her final inspection for everyone to approve before being sent.

New Business: The supervisors reviewed and approved the update ATV Ordinance 2025-1. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to adopt ordinance 2025-1, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The PSATS Conference May 4, 2024-May 7 2025 will be attended by all three supervisors. The secretary will register/make hotel reservations on January 14, 2025.

William Nale report issuing zero permits. Evan Brubaker inquired about an addition to his home for his in-laws. He stated he is drilling a new well, and having a new sewage system installed. The supervisors didn't have a problem with what he has planned. An email was received from Paradise Energy Solutions concerning a solar system to be installed on Nelson Martin's property for his chicken houses. The supervisors stated because of the KW size a zoning hearing will be necessary. An application for the zoning hearing is being sent.

Lost Creek Community Park: Nothing to discuss.

Oakand Mills Sewer: A letter will be sent to Patricia Bowersox concerning her final inspection. Christopher Strawser stated he is doing the testing now. The supervisors discussed having The Eads Group/Lucas take over handling issues with the sewer plant.

Lucas Parkes: The supervisors met with Lucas and PPL concerning the Green Light Go project. The LSA Grant was submitted in November. The multimodal grant will be decided in July.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting February 12, 2025 at 6 pm.
