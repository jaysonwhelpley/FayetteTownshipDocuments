# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, November 13, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD NOVEMBER 13, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nalem, Seth Mosebey, Jim King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Christopher Strawser stated Gabe Heckert discussed the $20.00/person fee for ALS service. Fayette Township has adjusted their budgeted amount for Port Royal ALS to $15,000.00. All county memberships are going to be raised next year. Kerry Tyson emailed that the retention pond at the Oakland Mills sewer plant is over specifications, holds more than is required.

New Business: The supervisors adopted Resolution 4-2024 for the LSA Grant Application Lucas Parkes is preparing for us. This grant is for funding of Leonard Road improvements. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to adopt, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the proposed 2025 budget. The proposed budget was adopted as presented. Final budget approval will be at the December 11, 2024 meeting. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to adopt, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors adopted Resolution 5-2024 for the Tax Levy, no tax increases were made. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to adopt, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The loan modification agreement was tabled for a future meeting. A new document is being prepared by Mette Evans of Harrisburg.

William Nale reported issuing six permits. Conner Clayton for Ben King-64x52 pole building, David Groff(3-permits) trailer hookups, Steve Ehrenzeller-demolish 5 structures, Trinity Solar for Roger Monroe-roof mounted solar. Patti Bowersox still hasn't had a final inspection. Christopher Strawser stated if it isn't done by the end of the year he will contact Commonwealth Code. Cody Shellenberger didn't get a permit for his building. Christopher Strawser is going to contact Commonwealth Code.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant: Christopher Strawser stated there is an issue with the UV light, Cory Fronk stated are test results are good, not to worry about replacing it at this time. Whitey is still doing the testing. Lucas Parkes has the paperwork prepared for additional hookups. Any hookups in the future will have a hookup fee which needs to be determined. Christopher Strawser stated we need to decide if we're going to use our township engineer to handle future issues. Seth Mosebey is going to send a letter to the resident that has a past due account.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas wasn't in attendance, Christopher Strawser spoke with him regarding the Green Light Go project. Lucas/French have the plans ready, he is going to set up a conference call with PennDOT to go over the plans. Craig Strawser is going to contact PPL about relocating the pole at the square. Lucas submitted the multimodal grant for Leonard Road for $500,000.00 with a 30% match. The LSA grant is due November 30, 2024. Lucas reviewed the paperwork for road studies on Musser Road. He will prepare an ordinance to implement the change of the stop sign to be stop except right turn.

Christopher Strawser questioned Seth Mosebey if he has had any further contact from the person wanting to install solar panels on the Drew Saner property in Bunkertown. Seth stated he hasn't heard from them. Seth Mosebey discussed the benefits of adopting Resolution 6-2024 for condemnation of the bank/vacant lot purchase to avoid transfer taxes. Craig Strawser made the motion to adopt the resolution, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Seth Mosebey is also going to update our ATV Ordinance 2017-3 to include insurance is required to use township roads.

Christopher Strawser discussed the email from the McAlisterville Alliance Committee. He is going to set up a meeting next Wednesday to discuss the issues.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting December 11, 2024 at 7 pm.
