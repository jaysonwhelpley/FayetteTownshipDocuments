181 BUNKERTOWN RD OCTOBER 14, 2026 6PM

The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey, Logan Weaver, Jim King, Bill Sarge, Lowell Shirk.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: The supervisors discussed the Zimmerman Plumbing,Heating,Electric invoice, it was decided to pay the invoice the next time checks are written. Nothing more was heard from Charles Stine regarding an issue with buildings on his property.

New Business: Bill Sarge was present with the Jerry/Rose Auker plan. The plan is subdividing a lot on Musser Rd to create a single family lot. The plan was approved, Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Logan Weaver and Lowell Shirk were present to discuss the plans for Lost Creek Country Market. The comments received from Lucas Parkes were addressed. The waiver for landscaping was discussed. After discussion it was decided with the landscaping planned with the islands in the parking lot, plus 10% planned for the front of the building a waiver wouldn't be required. The supervisors had approved the waiver for the curbing along the public roadway at the September meeting. The number of parking spaces was discussed. With the initial plans for the market 370 parking spaces will be sufficient. PennDot has traffic, intersection, crash, speed limit studies to complete. DEP NPDES permit is in process. The supervisors gave conditional approval pending, PennDot, DEP, Lucas's comments being addressed. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Final approval will be after PennDot studies are addressed, DEP NPDES approval.

Adoption of the Data Center Ordinance 4-2026 was deferred to our November meeting. It was discovered the Juniata County Planning Commission has to review the ordinance.

The Federal Surplus purchase application was reviewed and signed by the supervisors.

William Nale reported issuing 14 permits. Amos Halligan-40x64x16 porch w/roof, Gary Sheaffer-roof-mount solar, Stephen Nolt-22x120 lean to, Robin Schell-roof-mount solar, Mose Esh-12' add on barn, Mose Esh-replace 3 office sheds with one building, Andrew Kerstetter-30x40 pole building, Tyler Hepner-26x26 home addition, Patricia Erikson-new single family home, Joe Hepner-Set 14x70 single wide home. Home at the end of Billyville Rd is going to be torn down to build a school for handicapped children. The driveway was discussed, they will be talking to the supervisors concerning the placement.

Lost Creek Community Park: The restrooms will be closed for the season on October 19th. Craig Strawser suggested getting Yoder painting to look at the roof on pavilion #2. The supervisors will look into getting cameras installed at the park since there has been some damage recently. Christopher Strawser stated the light at the end of the ball field is on all the time. Craig stated they will check it out.

Oakland Mills Sewer: The new UV light arrived they will install in next two weeks. Christopher Strawser shut the UV lights down three days. Jim Connor stated it might be the circuit board that controls the UV lights. Samantha Beward's OM sewer bill was returned. Seth Mosebey is going to check if he can find her address. Christopher Strawser stated the flow numbers were up, he thinks the Beward property

The supervisors approved the 2026 MMO Pension Payment check for $11,576.00.

The secretary printed the 2026 budget to date for the supervisors to review in preparation of the 2027 budget.

Christopher Strawser questioned Jim King concerning townships contributing to the Port Royal EMS. Jim wasn't sure, but stated townships that don't support the PR EMS will have to pay a higher subscription fee. The supervisors discussed the Port Royal EMS check, last year we sent $32,000.00. The secretary is to issue a check next time checks are written.

Lucas Parkes: We didn't receive the LSA Grant for bank demo, the deadline to reapply is November 30th. After discussion it was decided to reapply for the bank demo. Sunset Dr grant with DCED has 10% match will be submitted July 2027. For Rockland Rd we could apply for Retro-reimbursement. The invoices are paid by the township and then PennDot reimburses 80%. Quarry Rd-looking at deeds/survey, will get information to Seth. Leonard Rd-need engineering agreement, full survey, PennDOT HOP, utility coordination, NPDES permit, road relocation plan, traffic control plan, right of way plan, design completion, bid process. Lucas estimated engineering cost for the project could be $100,000.00-$125,000.00. The closing of the NPDES permit for Oakland Mills Sewer is ready to submit to Hannah.

The supervisors discussed the situation with Livingston/Lonnie Snyder. Christopher Strawser is going to check with Lonnie where the eviction process stands.

Christopher Strawser stated he submitted the advertisement for the Zoning/Permit officer position.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting November 11, 2026 at 6 pm.
