# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, March 12, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD MARCH 12, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Hannah Hunsberger, Becky Lock.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Nelson Martin zoning hearing was held, the variance was granted for the solar system on his poultry farm. A copy of the decision was sent to Nelson Martin. The supervisors discussed the cinder bins. They would like to complete the updates before we receive salt for the coming winter.

New Business: Hannah Hunsberger, Juniata County Conservation District was present to discuss some environmental laws. Hannah also discussed the meeting on April 29th, topics covered will be solar development, sediment/erosion control, NPEDS permits & dirt/gravel roads. Lunch will be provided, supervisors need to RSVP attendance by April 8th. Christopher Strawser questioned requirements for loggers to get permits for sediment erosion plans. Hannah stated it depends on the acreage involved.

The supervisors reviewed the PSATS Defined Benefit Pension Plan adoption agreement. The motion to adopt was made by Robert Barton Jr., Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors adopted the 2025 Hazard Mitigation Plan Resolution 2025-2. The motion was made by Robert Barton, Jr. Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing 4-permits. Colby Dressler-addition to home, Lamar Hursh-roof mounted solar, Nelson Martin-roof mounted solar on his chicken house. William Nale reported in 2024 there were 14 new homes built in the township.

The supervisors discussed roads to pave/tar & chip. Craig Strawser will contact Matt Kenepp to schedule a day to check the roads.

Lost Creek Community Park: Whitey is rolling the fields in preparation to mow. The youth soccer teams fertilized the soccer fields. The restrooms will probably be opened mid-April.

Oakland Mills Sewer: The dissolved oxygen sensor seems to be working, Christopher Strawser is checking with Cory Fronk tomorrow. Christopher Strawser found out Terry Colyer is living with a daughter in Lewistown. Seth Mosebey stated we should just let the lien as is, when foreclosure occurs we will get what is owed. Patricia Bowersox hasn't completed the required work on her residence. Christopher Strawser is going to contact Commonwealth Code. Orenco hasn't responded about the size pump required for the Evangelical Methodist Church to hookup. Christopher Strawser is going to check with Kerry Tyson on how many/size of tanks and how often pumping will be required. Lucas is renewing the discharge permit. Lucas is also working on the guidelines for additional hookups.

The Eads Group: Lucas Parkes has the Green Light Go plans being reviewed by French Engineering before submitting to PennDOT. The pole at the square will be moved soon. The township was awarded $800,000.00 for the Leonard Road project from DCED Multimodal grant. The township has the structure permit. The PennDOT multimodal grant and the LSA grant applications are still pending. The supervisors/William Nale will meet with Steve Nolt in two-three weeks. Seth Mosebey will prepare/advertise the ordinance for the Musser Road stop sign for the April meeting.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting April 9, 2025 at 6 pm.
