# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, December 10, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD DECEMBER 10, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Becky Lock, Jim King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

New Business: After discussing the change in employment of George Lane, the supervisors appointed Hamilton & Musser, PA, CPAs for the 2025 audit of Fayette Township. The motion was made by Robert Barton, Jr. with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Craig Strawser was out plowing snow until later in the meeting.

The supervisors reviewed the proposed 2026 budget. The budget was approved with no changes or tax increase. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the new GSS Tax Software being implemented by the county. If the tax collector doesn't use the system, the courthouse employees would have to manually enter all he information into the system. Training will be sometime in January. There will be a monthly fee to the township of $50.00 for the software. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing two permits. Brent Brubaker-24x32 pole building, Rick Cluck-roof mounted solar panels. Christopher Strawser is going to have Herb Stoner check out a situation on Town Ridge, supposedly someone is living in a garage. Herb needs to check the sewage situation. Christopher Strawser mentioned Rick Cluck is having a property issue with is neighbor. Rick Cluck is going to have to hire his own attorney/surveyor.

Lost Creek Community Park: The supervisors discussed the rates for park rentals. It was decided to keep them all the same with the exception of the entire park rental fee. The fee for the entire park will change to $200.00. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Christopher Strawser wants to check with Craig about lights not working at the park.

Oakland Mills Sewer: Patti Bowersox has promised to pay $200.00 December 19th and $200.00 by December 30th. Also she has promised to pay the remaining balance by January 31, 2026. Everything is running good at the plant.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas reported they are working on the plan to vacate Quarry Rd. Christopher Strawser stated the supervisors along with Lucas will meet with Lost Creek Ag owners sometime in January to discuss the Leonard Rd project.

Christopher Strawser is going to get a sample floodplain ordinance to Seth Mosebey to work on. Christopher Strawser also spoke with PennDot concerning the site issue on Billyville/Cuba Mills road. PennDot might possibly install a mirror if that will help.

The secretary mentioned the 2026 Organizational meeting will be January 5, 2026 at 6 pm, the auditors will meet January 6, 2026 at 6 pm.

Jim King mentioned the Fayette Fire Company awards banquet will be April 19, 2026 at 1:00 pm.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting January 14, 2026 at 6 pm.
