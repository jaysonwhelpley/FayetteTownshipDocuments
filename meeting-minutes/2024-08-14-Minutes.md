# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, August 14, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD AUGUST 14, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Christopher Strawser stated he called Pam Herzing to report no information why her property is zoned commercial/residential. Christopher Strawser suggested she call the assessment office to make sure her tax billing is residential. Christopher Strawser also reported no luck locating the letter from CCD regarding the sewer lines crossing his property for the church.

New Business: The supervisors discussed the website content. They would like the Zoning Ordinance/Map, Land/Subdivision Ordinance, and the Solar Ordinance on the website. William Nale stated it was okay to list his contact information on the website using his business cell phone number.

William Nale reported issuing three permits. Patricia Bowersox-set 14x70 mobile home, Vonda Treaster-replace deck on the back of residence, Steve Ehrenzeller-24x36 prebuilt shed. Christopher Strawser stated he received a call from someone interested in purchasing the property across from the high school. There are concerns about the septic system. Jeff Frymoyer installed the system, he stated he had a permit.

Lost Creek Community Park: Whitey Frontz has spoken with the individual that was causing issues with the men's restroom. He will monitor the situation. Christopher Strawser stated he is going to speak with Tammy Brackbill about some issues with the fryers and restrooms during the carnival. The issues with the water drainage at the #3 pavilion are okay with the improvements made.

Oakland Mills: Whitey Frontz has been doing the testing, while Christopher Strawser is filling in for Dennis Lukens. Christopher Strawser discovered the sewer system needed to be registered for PA One Call to be notified when someone is digging in the area. The application for PA One Call was submitted. When the application is approved the mapping will be done for the area to be included in the PA One Call system. Christopher Strawser stated the requirements to add additional residents to the system. Seth Mosebey reviewed the process he suggests to collect past due sewer accounts. Establish a threshold amount, he suggests $500.00. The first step is to send a demand letter with a deadline for payment, usually thirty days. If that doesn't get their attention file a municipal lien with payment within a reasonable time. The next step is to get sheriff service on them with a 10 day notice after 20 days get a writ of execution to go after personal property. The township would recover the legal fees from the customer. The supervisors decided to proceed with the $500.00 threshold. Seth Mosebey is going to send Mrs. Kane a demand letter.

Lucas Parkes: The Green Light Go project was discussed. Lucas is trying to get a meeting scheduled with PennDOT to discuss the options available to move forward with the project. Lucas presented three options to PennDot to review prior to the meeting. Option #1-work to upgrade the signal heads, and signal control panel utilizing the existing signal posts and staying within the existing ROW. Option #2-The township purchases the former bank property, upgrades the traffic signal to one new signal post on the bank corner, and complete additional upgrades as funds allow. Option #3-we review the project as a full intersection betterment project for coordination with PennDOT. The grant application for the Leonard Road project was submitted.

Correspondence: The supervisors discussed the request by the Juniata Valley Veteran's Council to hold the Veteran's Day Parade in McAlisterville on November 9, 2024. The supervisors approved the request.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting September 11, 2024 at 7 pm.
