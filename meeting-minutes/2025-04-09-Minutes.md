# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, April 9, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD APRIL 9, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Becky Lock, Charles Tini, Tim Strong, Brent Musser, Jessica Guyer, George King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Christopher Strawser reported registering everyone for the April 29th JCCD meeting.

New Business: Jessica Guyer attended to present the township with the America 250PA municipal supporter signs. Jessica also discussed the possibility of holding an event in June of 2026 at the Lost Creek Community Park. The event is in the initial planning stage, she was gathering information on availability, fees, etc.

Brent Musser was present with the Martin/Frymoyer lot addition plan. The plan is to eliminate an encroachment of Martin's garage. The supervisors approved the waivers with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the road paving project for 2025. Paving will be done on People's Rd, Town Ridge Rd, and Musser Rd. It was decided to have Matt Kenepp prepare the bid package for advertisement. It was also decided to bid the materials bid for stone the same amounts as last year. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing eight permits. Paradise Energy/Martin-roof mounted solar, 2-Trinity Solar/Edwin Forry-roof mounted solar, Good News Lutheran Brethern-pavilion w/kitchen,storage,ADA restroom, Bryant/Jennifer Kantz-new home, Trinity Solar/Fred Raish-roof mounted solar, Aaron Petersheim-partial replacement/expansion of existing granite shop, Stephen Nolt-relocate business sign. William Nale also discussed the situation at the square of McAlisterville. Charles Tini stated the apartment building now belongs to Tim Strong. In the future Strong wants to build a garage next to the apartments(Seth Mosebey will check if allowed). Currently Strong is working on cars, the supervisors would like to see his operation enclosed or screened so it isn't visible from Route 35. The vacant lot which is also being used to work on cars also needs cleaned up. The supervisors informed Charles Tini they would give him two months to get the property cleaned up, also requested possibly installing a fence. Both properties discussed require permitting.

The supervisors reviewed the ordinance for the Musser Rd stop sign Ordinance 2025-2. Musser Rd will now have a stop except right turn sign. Ordinance 2025-2 was adopted with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the township's solar ordinance. The supervisors met with a solar company to discuss the possibility of installing solar panels on 24 acres of agricultural land in the township. According to our solar ordinance it isn't permitted on agricultural land without amending the ordinance. The supervisors aren't interested in amending the solar ordinance.

The letter received from the Port Royal ALS/EMS was discussed. The township budgeted $15,000.00 to them. Because of recent changes to the ALS/EMS they want to assess a $10.00 per resident fee in each township/borough. Fayette Township residents count is 3340, which means our fee would increase to $33,400.00. The motion to approve was made by Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr. second. Christopher Strawser abstained voting as he is a member of the Port Royal ALS/EMS committee.

Lost Creek Community Park: The restrooms will be opened on April 14, 2025.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant: Christopher Strawser reported changing the dissolved oxygen pump, he will be sending the old one to Orenco to be refurbished, Orenco will call with an estimate. Christopher Strawser stated it looks like Patricia Bowersox completed the work on her residence. She still hasn't contacted Commonwealth Code for an inspection. Christopher Strawser will call Bud at Commonwealth Code to make sure she schedules the final inspection.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas wasn't in attendance but reported to Christopher Strawser the Green Light Go final design is with French Engineering, should go to PennDot soon. The next step is the bidding process. The LSA Grant for one million for Leonard Rd. will be determined in September 2025. Lucas has completed the discharge permit for Oakland Mills. Lucas is working on the information for additional hookups to the sewer system. Lucas is also working on the Granny Flats documents.

George King was present with an update listing for the fire police. George also requested if signs could be put up prior to the Fayette Carnivals parade on Route 35. Craig Strawser stated he would contact Fulkroad's as they provided them in the past.

The supervisors briefly discussed the option to vacate Quarry Rd. The supervisors decided to table the discussion until the May meeting.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting May 14, 2025 at 6 pm.
