# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, December 11, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD DECEMBER 11, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Becky Lock.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Christopher Strawser stated Patricia Bowersox hasn't done anything for her final inspection. Seth Mosebey is going to do a post office inquiry to get the proper address, then he will send her a letter.

New Business: The supervisors reviewed the 2025 budget. No changes were made to the proposed budget. The budget was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors agreed to appoint Boyer & Ritter LLC for the 2024 audit. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the revised ATV ordinance. Seth Mosebey will have the revised ordinance advertised and ready to be adopted for our January 8, 2025 meeting. It will be ordinance 2025-1.

The loan modification agreement, Resolution 2024-7 was adopted. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing three permits. Simply Solar/Laurie Schreiber-roof mounted solar, Trinity Solar/Yvonne Geibel-roof mounted solar, Logan Walter-30x40x12 garage.

Lost Creek Community Park: The restrooms have been winterized and closed for the season. Craig Strawser stated Jeff Burd has offered to be in charge on the fryers for the carnival.

Oakland Mills: Whitey is still doing the testing, Christopher Strawser stated he will take over in a few weeks.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas submitted the extension letter to PennDot for the Green Light Go project. The project is expected to be completed by the spring of 2026. Lucas submitted the LSA grant November 30, 2024.

Craig Strawser is going to call Kurachick to come check out the traffic signal. There have been repeated issues of the traffic signal not working when it is raining.

Robert Barton Jr. suggested changing the meeting time to 6:00. The change will be voted on at the Organizational meeting on January 6, 2025 at 7 pm.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting January 8, 2025 at 6 pm.
