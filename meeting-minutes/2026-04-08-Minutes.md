181 BUNKERTOWN RD APRIL 8, 2026 6 PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey, Dan Smith, Becky Lock, Michael Szostek, Jim King.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: Dan Smith was present to inform the supervisors he is applying to the Department of Agriculture to sell frozen meat at his roadside stand. He is going to relocate the stand, it will be further away from the road. The supervisors didn't have any issues with his plan. Craig Strawser made the motion to approve, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Christopher Strawser stated he hasn't heard from PennDot concerning the sight issue on Cuba Mills Road. The Culbertson's discussed issues with their tax issue of paying taxes in Fayette Township. The secretary suggested talking with the tax collector to see if everything was worked out. Christopher Strawser stated he didn't get the application for Marcellus Shale Grant filled out. It was discovered we should have passed a resolution prior to applying. Lucas Parkes stated he didn't get a chance to work on the cinder bin repairs yet.

New Business: Michale Szostek was present to discuss his plans to form a business(Sandstone Ranch LLC) to sell boulders off his 11 acre property on Cobel Path, McAlisterville. He stated he won't be doing any excavating. The supervisors approved his plan. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed and approved Resolution 5-2026 for the Interim tax collector(Lori Diehl) effective June 11, 2026. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed meeting with Matt Kenepp tomorrow morning at 9:00 to check out roads to pave for 2026. It was also mentioned about the power line coming that will affect Musser, McMeen, Peoples, Benner and Long roads.

The supervisors reviewed the quote from National Gunite for the Benner Road culvert rehabilitation. The quote was for $55,197.00. The supervisors decided to go ahead with the project. Craig Strawser made the motion to approve, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing five permits. Seth Feltman-addition to home(in-law suite), Scott Scholl-20x28 pavilion, Eric Brubaker-30x36 pole shed, Jayson Whelpley-add second floor to home, Sue Wagner-12x20 deck, Donald Bell-two story home 2800 sq ft.

Lost Creek Community Park: Phil Holesapple started this week. The McAlisterville Alliance removed the Christmas tree from the stage.

Oakland Mills: The generator has water in the oil pan. Craig is going to check it out. There is a Cummins dealer in Centre Hall that could possibly repair. Lucas is working on the closure of the permit for JCCD.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas presented the final pay application for Herr Signal & Lighting for the Green Light Go traffic signal project. The balance due is $149,553.80 pending PennDOT's inspection. Craig Strawser made to motion to approve the payment of $149,553.80 to Herr Signal & Lighting, Robert Barton second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The secretary will make the payment next time she is printing checks.

Lucas is revising the Leonard Road layout per the field meeting. Hopefully the project will be ready for bidding in 2027. The grant received was for $800,000.00 with 20% match of $160,000.00. Rockland Road-waiting for grant results, should hear soon. Quarry Road-Lucas is getting paperwork to Seth Mosebey. LSA Grant for bank demolition-haven't heard anything yet.

The UCC ordinance will be adopted at the the May 13, 2026 meeting.

Craig Strawser discussed AI data centers. Seth Mosebey is going to search for information and look into a possible ordinance.

A complaint was received concerning resident Zach Patterson on Town Ridge. He has chickens on his property but doesn't have them confined to his property. Christopher Strawser will get a letter ready for the secretary to send.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting May 13, 2026 at 6 pm.
