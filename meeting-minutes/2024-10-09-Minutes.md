# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, October 9, 2024

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD OCTOBER 9, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Port Royal EMS/ALS-Can't do a Per Capita tax. It would have to be a referendum on the ballot, voted on by the general public if not passed we can assess ½ mil tax increase. The committee is going to present the issue at the county convention next month. If additional funding isn't generated the ALS will be in financial trouble in a few years. Craig Strawser stated he hasn't heard anything on the line painting.

The supervisors approved the Volunteer Firefighters Relief payment of $21,198.47. The motion was made by Robert Barton, Jr., Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors approved the 2024 Pension MMO payment of $10,451.00. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing 6 permits. ATMR, LLC-extension, Brandon Werner-new home, Nelson/Tim Lauver-42x54 farm shop/shed, Kevin Rickenbaugh/Slim Valley Rot'n Gun Club-24x30 cabin, Mitchell Aronson/Donna Milliken-roof mounted solar, Hess Lawn Care/Ronald Dreese-retaining wall, Amos Peachey-36x60 pole building/horse barn/storage.

Lost Creek Community Park: The carnival fee was discussed, it was decided to keep the fee at $4,000.00. It was also decided to keep the $400.00 credit towards next year's fee. Christopher Strawser wants to attend the carnival committee's meeting to discuss some issues from this year's carnival.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant: Christopher Strawser stated the flows have been good. Christopher Strawser stated he emailed Kerry Tyson concerning the retention pond required measurments. The retention pond measurements are in question, Christopher Strawser wants to make sure they are what the construction plans show. We also discussed a customer's account that is past due & over $500.00. It was decided since the quarterly billing just went out to give the opportunity for payment until the November meeting.

Lucas Parkes is working on the ordinance for additional hookups for the next meeting. Christopher Strawser stated the appraisals for the bank/vacant lot haven't been received yet. They should be received by Friday, October 11, 2024. The deadlines for the grant applications were discussed. The multimodal is due November 5, 2024, the LSA grant is due November 30, 2024.

The secretary will call Ginger Best with the number attending the County Convention. Four will be attending from Fayette Township, Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., William Nale.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting November 13, 2024 at 7 pm.
