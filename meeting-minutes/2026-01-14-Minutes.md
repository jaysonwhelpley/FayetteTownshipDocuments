181 BUNKERTOWN RD JANUARY 14, 2026 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Lucas Parkes, Jim King, Anthony Willis, Brent Musser, Rick Cluck.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton, Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Old Business: Christopher Strawser didn't have time to print the floodplain ordinance information. He will get the information to Seth Mosebey to work on our ordinance.

Rick Cluck was present to discuss his property line issues. The supervisors advised him to hire an attorney and also to have a survey completed.

New Business: Brent Musser was present with the following sewage planning modules, Anthony Willis, Buddy Manevel, & Roger Shallenberger. Resolution 2026-1, Dalton Shipp, Resolution 2026-2, Roger Shallenberger, Resolution 2026-3, Buddy Maneval were adopted, motion made by Craig Strawser, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Brent also had the Michael Brubaker subdivision plan-the purpose of the plan is to correct the lot his house is on. He owns all the property but the house was put on the wrong parcel.

William Nale reported issuing 5 permits, Jay Graybill-welding shop 125x70, Tom Feltman-roof mounted solar, Juniata Co. School District-East Juniata-accessible restroom in existing structure, Carter Smith-hunting stand, Heather Price-roof mounted solar, Robt. Lehman/Almanzo Diem-16x46 addition.

The supervisors are going to meet with Steve/Lost Creek Ag tomorrow morning.

Lost Creek Community Park: The supervisors discussed hiring a park maintenance person. The secretary stated that the supervisors might want to consider paying the individual from the General Fund and issuing a 1099 at the end of the year. Christopher Strawser is going to get an ad ready for the Juniata Sentinel. The carnival committee has changed vendors for the amusement rides since there was a schedule conflict with Bartlebaugh's. This will enable them to keep the original dates for the carnival. Craig Strawser questioned what could be done with a large hickory tree next to the park. Christopher Strawser stated the Jerry Auker wants the tree if we cut it down.

Oakland Mills: The tenant that has a delinquent balance has paid another $200.00 with the balance of $741.00 to be paid by January 31, 2026. Christopher Strawser questioned the status of the sheriff sale of Terry Colyer's property. Seth Mosebey stated it doesn't have to be advertised again, but he hasn't heard anything from the Sheriff's office. Christopher Strawser stated he is going to get a divider and install a toilet at the plant. Christopher Strawser stated he needs to get Lepley Electric to check the UV lights.

Lucas Parkes: Lucas stated he has the paperwork to closeout the NPDES permit for the Oakland Mills Sewer plant. The stormwater plan needs to be recorded as certified by Kerry Tyson. The rules/regulations sample ordinance for additional hookups to the sewer system is ready for the supervisors to review for approval/changes. The fee schedule related to additional hookups is almost finished. Leonard Rd project-supervisors are meeting with Steve at Lost Creek Ag tomorrow morning at 9:00 am. LSA Grant for the bank demo was submitted in November. Lucas is going to check on the phone company moving the wires for the traffic light installation. Lucas is going to get Herr Signal & Lighting invoice of $58,516.00 on an official pay application, withholding 5%. PennDot will reimburse 80%. The payment goes directly to Herr Signal & Lighting. The supervisors approved the payment with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. PennDot will want a copy of front/back of the check. Lucas is getting the information together for the surveyors to work on the Quarry Rd.

Lucas stated PennDot opened up multimodal grant applications. The supervisors decided to get some of projects already in progress finished before starting any more projects. Didn't receive anything on the DCED grant for Rockland Rd.

The supervisors want to meet with Jason Nace before the next meeting.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting February 11, 2026 at 6 pm.
