# Fayette Township Board of Supervisors — Meeting Minutes, February 12, 2025

Source: https://fayettetownship.com/meetings/2024-minutes/ (web text-extraction).

---

181 BUNKERTOWN RD FEBRUARY 12, 2025 6PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Robert Barton Jr., Craig Strawser, Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Lucas Parkes, Seth Mosebey.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: Christopher Strawser reported the Nelson Martin zoning hearing will be held February 25, 2025 at 7:30 pm.

New Business: The supervisors reviewed and signed the Juniata Sewage Committee Resolution 2025-1 and the Articles of Cooperation. Craig Strawser made the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors reviewed the information from Mike Hower for naming streets/roads. Craig Strawser is going to keep the information on file for future reference.

William Nale reported issuing two permits. Nathan Yoder-addition to home, Aaron Petersheim-36 x 60 workshop. William Nale reported he is waiting on permit/checks for several solar systems.

Lost Creek Community Park: nothing to report.

Oakland Mills Sewage Plant: The supervisors discussed the church hooking up to the sewer system. Orenco is working on information for oxygen pump replacement. Christopher Strawser reported Patti Bowersox has half of the skirting on her trailer. It was decided to keep an eye on the progress until the next meeting. Seth Mosebey is suggesting we continue the lien/filing the complaint on the Terry Colyer property. If the mortgage company forecloses, our lien is on file at the courthouse, the township will get paid. The supervisors decided not to continue billing Terry Colyer for future quarters since he moved out. They will monitor the property for activity.

Lucas Parkes: GP11 permit approved for Leonard Road. Green Light Go-comments from Tate being reviewed by French, updating ROW plan. Lucas thinks the project should be ready for bidding early fall. Musser Road stop sign change is drafted, he will send to the township and Seth Mosebey. Lucas is working on the Granny Flats changes, possibly have them register once a year to monitor usage. Craig Strawser discussed posting weight limits on Sunset Road. Lucas is going to work on the renewal NPDES discharge permit for the plant needs submitted by June 3, 2025.

The supervisors discussed working on the cinder bin. Craig Strawser stated the stock level is low, he doesn't want to get more if the work is going to be done this spring/summer.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting March 12, 2025 at 6 pm.
