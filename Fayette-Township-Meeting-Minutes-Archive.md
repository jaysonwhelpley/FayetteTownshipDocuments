## June 12, 2024

181 BUNKERTOWN RD JUNE 12, 2024 7PM
The Fayette Township Board of Supervisors held their regular monthly meeting with the following present: Christopher Strawser, Craig Strawser, Robert Barton, Jr., Sharon Lukens, William Nale, Seth Mosebey, Brent Musser.

After the meeting was called to order, the minutes were reviewed and approved. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Public Comment: None

Old Business: None

New Business: Axis Surveying was present with the David Renno plan and the Michael Troyer plan. The purpose of the Renno plan is to subdivide Lot 1 from TMP 03-17-29 to create one residential building lot. The waivers were approved with Craig Strawser making the motion to approve, Robert Barton Jr second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The purpose of the Troyer plan is to subdivide Lot 1 from TMP 03-02-28 to create a single family residential building lot. The waivers were given conditional approval upon DEP approval. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was given conditional approval upon DEP approval. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The bids for the tar/chip project were opened. The tar/chip project was awarded to Russell Standard in the amount of $123,089.34. A copy of the bid results is attached. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to award the bid to Russell Standard, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The materials bid package was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc., the total bid package amount was $153,500.00. A copy of the bid results is attached. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to award the bid to Jay Fulkroad & Sons, Inc., Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the rehab of the culverts on Pine/Maple, and Peoples Rd. Craig Strawser researched the possibility of doing the work ourselves. It was decided there were too many obstacles on Pine/Maple with the water/sewer, electric, and telephone lines, cost of the permit to justify doing the work ourselves. It was decided to accept the proposal from National Gunite(Co-Stars) in the amount of $77,377.00. Craig Strawser is going to call to get the project started.

The supervisors discussed the paving of Pine/Maple. It was decided to bid the project. Craig is going to call Matt Kenepp to get the bid process started.

William Nale reported issuing 8 permits(1-free). Mike Eby-replace existing deck, Aaron Swarey-barn & buggy shed, new home, Cliff Swartwood-deck behind house, Urie Byler-40x44 horse/buggy barn, Jeff Yoder-addition to home, Charles Tini-repair fire damage to apartments, Robert Swartz-above ground pool, Jonathan Wileman-retaining wall. The request from Missy Culbertson for permission to use the Fayette Fire Company's kitchen to make baked goods was discussed. Missy stated she needs township approval. The question was asked where she would be selling the goods from. Christopher Strawser was going to contact her. William Nale questioned the Bowersox property in Oakland Mills. Christopher Strawser stated they haven't done anything yet. The former Brandt Trailer Park was discussed. The new owner wants to purchase additional property to install a bigger trailer. The supervisors stated anything new he installs has to meet zoning requirements.

Lost Creek Community Park- Christopher Strawser stated they will be taking the fryer out that doesn't work. They discussed possible work at pavilion #3.

Oakland Mills Sewer Plant-Christopher Strawser reported everything is running okay. It was decided to have Seth Mosebey follow up with letter sent to Sippel for punch list items.

Lucas Parks-The green light go project was discussed. PennDot has agreed to an extension for the project. However, Lucas stated with the added issues of the ADA ramps, and right of way issues he feels PennDot needs to reevaluate to project because the cost to do everything PennDot is asking is going to be well over the grant amount. Lucas stated by next week French Engineering will have information available. Lucas will be in contact with the supervisors on the plan moving forward. Lucas reported he is moving ahead with the permits for the Leonard Road project.

Seth Mosebey is going to move forward with the Sewage Ordinance, 10 acre exemption. He will advertise the ordinance for the July meeting.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements, and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting July 10, 2024 at 7 pm.

Christopher Strawser discovered the sewer system needed to be registered for PA One Call to be notified when someone is digging in the area. The application for PA One Call was submitted. When the application is approved the mapping will be done for the area to be included in the PA One Call system. Christopher Strawser stated the requirements to add additional residents to the system. Seth Mosebey reviewed the process he suggests to collect past due sewer accounts. If that doesn't get their attention file a municipal lien with payment within a reasonable time. The next step is to get sheriff service on them with a 10 day notice after 20 days get a writ of execution to go after personal property. The township would recover the legal fees from the customer. Option #2-The township purchases the former bank property, upgrades the traffic signal to one new signal post on the bank corner, and complete additional upgrades as funds allow. Option #3-we review the project as a full intersection betterment project for coordination with PennDOT. The grant application for the Leonard Road project was submitted.

Correspondence: The supervisors discussed the request by the Juniata Valley Veteran's Council to hold the Veteran's Day Parade in McAlisterville on November 9, 2024. The supervisors approved the request.

New Business: The paving and materials bids were opened. The paving bid was awarded to Glenn O. Hawbaker in the amount of $359,969.00. The materials bid was awarded to Jay Fulkroad & Sons. A copy of all bid results is attached.

Glenn Kerstetter was present with Resolution 2026-7 regarding the designation of Port Royal EMS/ALS service for Fayette Township. Glenn also presented a letter in support of Johnathan & Staci Spotts constructing a new retail building next to the firehouse. Robert Barton made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Axis Surveying-Brent Musser was present with the Lowell Shirk plan for the sewage module for DEP, Resolution 2026-8. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. Brent also presented a plan for the Lost Creek Golf Course to subdivide the Club House +4 acres from the golf course. The waivers were approved with Craig Strawser making the motion, Robert Barton Jr. second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The plan was approved with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

Resolution 2026-6 giving the new tax collector the authority to open a new checking account was adopted. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to adopt, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous. The supervisors also approved reimbursing Lori Diehl $200.00 for her training and $175.00 for a printer she purchased from Bev Richmond(former tax collector).

Johnathan and Staci Spotts were present to discuss their plan for a 12x28 shed for their home-based business of making spice mixes, jerky, and baked goods. A portion of the property is zoned commercial. The supervisors approved their plan with Robert Barton Jr. making the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the quote from Alpha Space Control for painting a single yellow line on 25 miles of township roads at a cost of $13,200.00. It was decided to request a quote on all the township roads since we didn't paint any lines last year. The new quote should be $26,400.00. Craig is going to request a new quote. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion to approve, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

The supervisors discussed the quote received from Landisburg Concrete Walls for the cinder bins. The quote was for $10,043.80. None of the other contacts responded to the request. It was decided to approve the quote. Robert Barton Jr. made the motion, Craig Strawser second, with Christopher Strawser making it unanimous.

William Nale reported issuing two permits. One of the permits was incomplete. Jay Nissley got a permit for a 32x48 shed.

The supervisors held a discussion on the township's data center ordinance. There were several township residents present to discuss their concerns of data centers in the township. The supervisors along with Seth Mosebey are in the process of developing an ordinance regarding data centers.

Lost Creek Park: The supervisors decided to let Jeff Hoover handle blocking the areas where no parking is allowed on his property for the two events scheduled at the park this weekend.

Oakland Mills Sewer: The supervisors approved the payment of the Cummins Sales & Service invoice for the generator. Going forward the township is going to use Breon's Inc. from Pleasant Gap, PA for all repairs to the generator. The new discharge permit hasn't been received yet. The UV light is having some issues that need looked into. Christopher Strawser spoke with Patti Bowersox about her account. She has promised to pay $200.00 bi-weekly starting in June.

Lucas Parkes didn't attend the meeting but Christopher Strawser spoke with him earlier today. Lucas is working on the final design for the Leonard Rd project. Lucas is hoping to have the project ready for bidding early 2027. The grant for the Rockland Rd project will be awarded in September. Lucas mentioned a multi-modal grant the township could apply for to do the bridge on Lost Creek/Sunset. Lucas also discussed bonding the following roads McMeen, Long, People, Hower, Benner, and Musser because of the electrical work that is taking place in the area. Seth Mosebey will possibly have an ordinance ready for our July meeting. Lucas is also going to get the information on Quarry Rd to Seth Mosebey.

Checks were signed, the paid invoice report, bank statements and financial statements were reviewed.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned with the next meeting July 8, 2026.
